# HBM NTM Reforged — 0.9.1-beta

**For players** · Minecraft 1.12.2

Thank you for testing the beta! Here is what changed since 0.9.0-alpha in plain language.

---

## Text & languages
- **Russian and other languages display correctly again.** Names and tooltips were showing broken symbols because of a wrong text encoding — that is fixed.
- English and Ukrainian strings were cleaned up too.

## Finding items more easily
- **Creative inventory tabs are sorted in a sensible order** — related items stay together instead of appearing randomly.
- **JEI shows HBM items in the right order**, including Space-addon fuel rods and other items that used to appear in the wrong place.
- Depleted RTG pellets no longer clutter JEI (you were not meant to craft with them anyway).

## Crafting
- **Many missing recipes are back**, including weapons, machine parts, blocks, and workbench crafts that existed in Extended Edition.
- You can now craft the **AMS Muzzle** component.

## New machine
- **AMS Emitter** is available — build it, open the GUI, and connect it to your AMS setup. The cryogel tank in the GUI behaves correctly now.

## Blast doors
- **Large sliding blast doors with keypads** no longer leave invisible “ghost” keypad blocks when you break them.
- Breaking these doors should also no longer cause huge lag spikes.

## Weapons
- **Shotgun meathook** releases properly when you switch items, die, or leave the world — no more stuck grappling.
- **Aiming while holding right mouse** works as expected on supported guns.
- Grenades and several guns appear in a more logical order in tabs and JEI.
- Small visual fixes on some gun models (e.g. Fat Man, SPAS-12).

## Everything from 0.9.0-alpha
This beta also includes the previous alpha update: stronger blast-resistant blocks, ITER and plasma machines, hazard and ore fixes, more fluids, and many recipe and multiblock improvements.
